Add it to her iPhone

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an up arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, keep Open as Web App on if shown, then tap Add.
  4. Open the new icon while online. Wait for Saved for offline, then try a sound in airplane mode.

Use the phone’s volume buttons for loudness. Tap another sound to switch, tap the playing sound to stop, or turn on Loop. Keep the app open while playing.

Make it yours

Replace a button with a recording from your Files app. Your recording stays on this device; it isn’t uploaded. Add it after opening the Home Screen app.

Sounds & credits

Announcements come from the FOIA.fm BART archive, a third-party collection of agency audio. The archive does not specify a reuse license. BART Party is an unofficial personal costume project.

Door beeps, horn, and tunnel sounds are original synthesized recreations, labeled on each button. Any “Demo voice” button is a temporary computer voice, not a BART recording.

For real train noise, “BART ride” by elemkr includes doors, arrivals, and departures (CC BY-NC 3.0; free account needed to download). You can add an excerpt above.
