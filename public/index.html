Tap a button to play. Tap it again to stop, or tap another to switch. Loop repeats the current sound. Use your iPhone’s volume buttons for loudness.

Add to your iPhone

  1. Open this site in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
  3. Keep Open as Web App on if shown, then tap Add.
  4. Open the icon while online. Wait for Saved for offline, then test in airplane mode.

Keep the app open while playing. Pair a speaker in your phone’s Bluetooth settings for extra volume.

Your own recording

Replace any button with a recording from Files. It stays on this device and works offline. Import inside the installed Home Screen app.
